Contributions and Feedback Welcome!

The Combinatorial Interaction Testing Repository is developed and maintained by Huayao Wu from Nanjing University.

This repository is periodically and automatically updated using the DBLP APIs to search for relevant papers and retrieve their metadata. We aim to keep the papers collected as comprehensive and accurate as possible. If you notice any missing papers, incorrect metadata, or have suggestions for improvement, please feel free to send me an email, or open an issue on GitHub.
